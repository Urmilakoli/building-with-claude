Mind-trick games

Two classic lab experiments you can run on yourself. Each one is worth bonus XP.

The Stroop test (attention)

Tap the ink colour, not the word. Your reading autopilot will fight you. 10 rounds.

Why it works: reading is so automatic that your brain reads the word before you can stop it. Overriding it costs time, which is attention at work.

The false memory test (memory)

Study 15 words for 15 seconds. Then pick every word you saw. Be honest.

Based on the Deese–Roediger–McDermott (DRM) experiment, 1995.
