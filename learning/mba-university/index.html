CAP 300 · Capstone: Ideas for Finance & Risk

6.5 ECTS · Term 3 · Due 14 Feb 2027

Write a one-page memo to a fintech or bank risk team that uses at least five concepts from five different courses to improve one real process. Ideas:

  1. AML alert fatigue: base-rate neglect (THK 102), behavioural nudges (ECO 201), machine learning precision and recall (TEC 210)
  2. Sanctions screening: economic sanctions and chokepoints (IRL 260), corruption and PEPs (GOV 250), misleading graphs (THK 102)
  3. Customer savings app: habits (PSY 101), loss aversion and defaults (ECO 201), human-computer interaction (TEC 210)

Assessment: clear problem (20%), correct use of concepts (40%), evidence and data (20%), clear writing (20%). Paste your memo, then ask Claude to grade it against this rubric.
