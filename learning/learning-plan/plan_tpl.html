Your 12-week plan to learn everything on your list

Every topic you sent, in one place: 3 short levels a day, 6 days a week, and a game-and-review day on Sundays. About 20 minutes a day.

How to use this plan

1. Open today's cardTap a topic to jump straight to its world in the lesson. Open the level, read the story and the big idea.
2. Answer the quizOne question per level. Getting it wrong is fine; the explanation is where the learning happens.
3. Do one "Try it today"Pick the one that fits your day. Doing beats reading.
4. Tick the dayTick it here when you finish. On Sundays, play that week's lesson games and retry any quiz you missed.

Missed a day? Don't double up. Just continue from the first unticked day; the plan waits for you. Ticks are saved in this browser only, so use the same device and browser each time.
